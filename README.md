# Orbit

Orbit is designed to work around **you**.

It stays in the background, constantly moving, adapting, and keeping things in balance. Orbit takes care of the tedious tasks of everyday life, reducing the mental load and freeing up your time and energy for what matters most.
From managing the little things you often forget to supporting your physical and mental wellbeing, Orbit quietly keeps everything moving so life feels less like something you have to manage, and more like something you can simply live.

**you** stay at the centre. 

Orbit takes care of the rest.
